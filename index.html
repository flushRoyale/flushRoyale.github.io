      How I think about risk

      I’m heavily influenced by Nassim Nicholas Taleb, and the idea that changed my play most was ruin. Ruin is permanent, so no edge justifies risking it. I set strict bankroll rules designed to avoid ruin.

      His work on randomness and fat tails also shapes how I read results: I ask how much of an outcome could be luck, which assumptions fail in the tails, and what being wrong would cost. Poker gave me a large sample to study, more than 500,000 hands, and I moved from game-theory-optimal play toward exploitative play. I want to keep asking the same questions in finance.
